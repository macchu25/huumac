# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Vietnamese friends and family receiving a university graduation invitation, most often opening it on a phone.

## Product Purpose
A personal invitation that unfolds like a physical letter. The primary action opens an envelope and reveals the graduation card with a short pop-out animation.

## Brand Personality
Ceremonial, heartfelt, tactile. Inspired by engraved university stationery.

## Design Principles
Make the letter the experience. Let motion build anticipation. Keep invitation details readable. Never invent personal event information; label missing details as placeholders.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts.
